# Club redesign surface brief

- **Scope:** Replace the visual system across the public Programming Club site and MariTools (`/tools`). Book Delivery (`/books`) is an independent service with its own brief and chrome; do not treat it as part of MariTools.
- **Visitor modes:** Persuade on the homepage, read and browse on club archives, operate during Book Delivery and checkout.
- **Audience:** Marianopolis students, including complete beginners and students buying required course books.
- **Primary action:** Sign up through the verified club member registration form.
- **Proof and content:** Programming Hub, peer help, workshops, mini-competitions, MariHacks, existing workshop and resource archives, real course lists and prices.
- **Constraints:** No beige, toy-like styling, excessive cards, decorative copy, or unverified dates. Instagram and Discord are icons. Cart appears only inside Book Delivery.
- **Chosen direction:** The Student Technical Journal, using the user's bookstore and two-column checkout references as craft anchors.
- **Memorable moment:** The first viewport reads like a decisive journal cover whose evidence continues immediately into the page; checkout becomes the same publication transformed into a precise payment workspace.
- **Motion:** Immediate press states, short navigation and cart continuity, explicit payment progress, and full reduced-motion support.
