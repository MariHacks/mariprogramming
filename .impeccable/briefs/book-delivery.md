# Book Delivery surface brief

- **Scope:** The standalone Book Delivery service at `/books` — catalogue, cart, checkout, confirmation, and request flows. Independent from MariTools (`/tools`).
- **Visitor mode:** Operate — students complete a purchase or book request without club recruitment chrome competing with the task.
- **Audience:** Marianopolis students buying required French and English course books.
- **Primary action:** Choose a teacher-course list, select books, pay (when live), and understand Wayne's Front Desk pickup.
- **Proof and content:** Real teacher-course lists, book covers, prices, bookstore links, pickup location.
- **Constraints:** Cart and Book Delivery navigation appear only on `/books` routes when live. No MariTools sidebar, no shared account requirement for guest checkout. Commerce controls never leak to club or MariTools pages.
- **Visual relationship:** May share Inter/Inter Tight and editorial grid discipline with the club site, but owns its own transactional density and checkout rail — not MariTools instrument chrome.
- **Motion:** Immediate press states, calm payment progress, full reduced-motion support.
