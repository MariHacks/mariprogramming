My schedule

Paste the numbered course list from Omnivox. You do not need to log in, and nothing is sent to the college.

How to copy your Omnivox list
  1. Open Omnivox and sign in with your student number. MariTools never asks for that number here.
  2. Open Course Schedule, pick the semester, then Obtain my schedule.
  3. Open the printer-friendly compact list and copy the numbered courses on the right. Skip the grid, name, and student number.

Accepted input

  1. Numbered compact list from the printer-friendly page.
  2. Course code, section, teacher, day, time, and room.
  3. Not the visual grid, and not the name block at the top.
Overlap on Tuesday. Programming I and a lab meeting both use 10:00–11:00. Export still works. Correct the room or time before you rely on the file.
