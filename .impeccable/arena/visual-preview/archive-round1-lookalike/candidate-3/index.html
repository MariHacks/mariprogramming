My schedule

Paste the compact Omnivox list. The timetable is built in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How to copy your Omnivox list
  1. Open Omnivox. Go to marianopolis.omnivox.ca and sign in with your student number there. MariTools never asks for that number.
  2. Course Schedule. In the left sidebar, click Course Schedule, pick the semester, then Obtain my schedule.
  3. Printer-friendly version. Open the yellow banner, choose Compact printable semester schedule, then View.
  4. Copy the list. Copy the numbered course list on the right. Skip the grid, and skip the name and student number at the top.

Accepted input

  • Compact printable semester schedule from Omnivox
  • Course codes, sections, teachers, days, times, and rooms
  • Paste stays on this device until you download a calendar
