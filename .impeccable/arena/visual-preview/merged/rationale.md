# MariTools merged preview

This preview treats MariTools as a campus instrument. The calendar carries the visual weight while the shell stays quiet enough to disappear during weekly use.

The site header follows the production hierarchy: club mark and name, About, Events, MariTools, contact and social destinations, then one account action. Signed-out students see only Sign up. Signed-in students see a compact identity control with Your account and Sign out. The preview bar toggles these mutually exclusive states.

The tools sidebar is new. It uses a pale, flat course-index treatment with small section labels, plain rows, a thin current marker, and no shortcuts or account controls. MariTools returns home; the footer returns to the club. Below 920px it becomes a full-height drawer with a scrim and close control.

Schedule restores Candidate 1's event fills, padding, radius, shadow, small type, lane positioning, current-time line, conflict treatment, and Omnivox drawer. The calendar keeps the roomier width. Google Calendar is the primary export action and opens an explanation popup. Both Google and Apple or Outlook `.ics` downloads live inside that popup.

The Omnivox tutorial copies the nine production steps from `src/lib/maritools/tutorial-steps.js`. A first import opens the guide, Done opens the paste drawer, and the opt-out is stored in localStorage. The preview picker can always reopen it for review. Labeled HTML mock frames stand in for missing cropped Omnivox screenshots and must be replaced with real crops before production use.

Free time is now a When2meet-style board. The grid supports pointer drag painting, common slots remain visible in green, and the right panel holds the share link, members, guest display name, schedule import, and save action. A separate boards index supports multiple created or joined boards. The no-login rule appears beside the grid and again on the index.

Semester retains Candidate 1's extraction review, including the editable assessment table, missing-date error, share checkbox, and Save action. The masthead is gone. Add course outline now sits in the course-list header, and the list and review sheet sit directly in the main pane.

Catalog, clubs, forum, and thread use Candidate 2's flat ruled patterns directly in the main pane. Club rows open a dense detail page with category, about, meeting rhythm, official links, and joining instructions. Account is a single settings column with ruled bands instead of a nested dashboard.

All content remains illustrative. The preview avoids Book Delivery, fake current events, decorative dashboards, and font changes that would conflict with the production design system.
