# MariTools merged preview

This preview treats MariTools as a campus instrument. The calendar carries the visual weight while the shell stays quiet enough to disappear during weekly use.

The site header follows the production hierarchy: club mark and name, About, Events, MariTools, contact and social destinations, Account, and Sign up. Account is the only redesigned part. Signed-out students get a direct Account link and a quiet Google option. Signed-in students get a compact identity control with Your account and Sign out. The preview bar toggles these states.

The tools sidebar is new. It uses a pale, flat course-index treatment with small section labels, plain rows, a thin current marker, and no shortcuts or account controls. MariTools returns home; the footer returns to the club. Below 920px it becomes a full-height drawer with a scrim and close control.

Schedule keeps Candidate 1's event geometry, current-time line, conflict treatment, and Omnivox drawer. The calendar receives more width by default and the utility bar follows Candidate 2's simple action order. Google Calendar is the primary export action; the raw ICS download remains quiet.

The Omnivox tutorial uses the nine production steps. A first import opens the guide, Done opens the drawer, and the opt-out is stored in localStorage. The preview picker can always reopen it for review.

Free time is now a When2meet-style board. The grid supports pointer drag painting, common slots remain visible in green, and the right panel holds the share link, members, guest display name, schedule import, and save action. A separate boards index supports multiple created or joined boards. The no-login rule appears beside the grid and again on the index.

Semester retains Candidate 1's extraction review, including the editable assessment table, missing-date error, share checkbox, and Save action. A compact course list adds the multi-course state without replacing the review sheet.

Catalog, clubs, forum, and thread use Candidate 2's strongest patterns: expandable catalog rows with assessment and book detail, a factual club directory, a dense discussion index, and a thread with a clear helpful-answer state.

All content remains illustrative. The preview avoids Book Delivery, fake current events, decorative dashboards, and font changes that would conflict with the production design system.
